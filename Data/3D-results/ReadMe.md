
# 3D results

## What is here

Representative 3D output material for wave-function evolution, detector interaction, and Bohmian-trajectory visualization.

## What to open first

- `Bohmian-Trajectories/` for trajectory release links.
- `TimeEvolution-WaveFunction-Gifs/` for GIF release links.

## What is reproducible

These folders support inspection of selected 3D outputs and media. Full reproduction of 3D simulations may require GPU/HPC runs and external data.

## What is legacy / heavy / HPC-dependent

Large `.npy`, `.npz`, zipped trajectory archives, and GIF collections should generally be released externally rather than committed wholesale.
