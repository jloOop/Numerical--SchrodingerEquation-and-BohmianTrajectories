# Selected 3D Visualizations and Single-Particle Evolution

This section presents a selected set of numerical results from the paper "…".  
It includes:

- 3D time evolution of a spinor wave packet in a finite box,  
- propagation towards and interaction with an absorbing boundary at the top (with and without spin-dependent boundary conditions),  
- and the evolution of a representative single particle (trajectory) associated with the spinor dynamics.

The animations (GIFs) visualize how the probability density and the particle motion evolve in time and eventually reach the absorbing boundary.
